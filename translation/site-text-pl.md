# agnieszka clarity — site text for translation (PL → EN)

The site will have two languages: **Polish (source)** and **English**. Every unique Polish text of the prototype is listed once below, with an ID. Where the same Polish sentence appears in several places on the site, it is listed once and the same English will be used everywhere.

## How to translate

- Write the English under each **EN:** line (or in the `EN (translation)` column of `site-text-pl.csv`; both files hold the same IDs).
- Keep the voice: warm, calm, personal; Polish speaks to one person informally ("Ci", "Twoje"), so use "you / your". Short sentences stay short.
- `<br>` means a forced line break inside a headline or paragraph. Keep a `<br>` in the English where the line should break; do not remove them all.
- Button labels and menu items must stay short (they sit in small buttons).
- Names stay as they are: agnieszka clarity, Brainspotting, Energy Medicine, Kundalini, Flow, Studio Jogi. Decide how to render the Polish service names (for example "Sesja indywidualna 1:1", "Moc mantry i wibracji") and use them consistently.
- Texts marked "(example data)" are placeholders (dates, prices, event names) and will change.
- Groups 1–3 are what visitors see now and are the priority. Group 4 is the older alternative version of the home page (kept as a fallback); group 5 was hidden from the home page by the client; group 6 are internal switches. They can be translated last.
